ENTRY — 002 How it works

Three modules. One running ledger.

  • LEARN

    Bite-sized financial fundamentals

    Budgeting, credit, taxes, and investing basics, taught through short interactive lessons — not a textbook chapter.

  • PRACTICE

    A simulated trading & investing engine

    Students get virtual capital and trade against real, live market data — same mechanics as a real brokerage, none of the real-money risk.

  • TRACK

    A personal ledger of every decision

    Every simulated trade and budgeting choice is logged, so students can see the actual consequences of their financial decisions over time.
